## Where the money actually goes

A 2BHK budget is not spread evenly. Two rooms eat more than half of it.

### Kitchen: 25–30%

A modular kitchen is priced per running foot (rft) of counter, and an Indore 2BHK kitchen is usually 10–13 rft in an L or parallel layout. At our Standard rate of ₹2,900/rft that is ₹29,000–38,000 for base and wall cabinets — before the counter, the sink, the chimney and the tall unit.

Add a granite counter (₹180–260/sq ft installed), a tall unit for the fridge recess, and loft storage above the wall cabinets (₹950/rft), and a real Standard kitchen settles at **₹2.4–3.2 lakh**.

The upgrades that move the number fastest are the shutter finish (laminate → acrylic adds ~₹600/rft; PU adds ~₹1,200/rft) and the hardware (soft-close everywhere is a ₹18,000–25,000 line item on its own).

### Wardrobes: 20–25%

Two bedrooms, two wardrobes. A 7 ft openable wardrobe at ₹2,200/rft is about ₹1.5 lakh for the pair, and lofts add another ₹13,000. Sliding wardrobes cost 25–30% more per rft because of the track hardware — we cover when they are worth it in [our wardrobe article](/journal/wardrobes-sliding-vs-openable-cost-per-running-foot/).

### False ceiling and lighting: 10–12%

A gypsum ceiling in the living room and both bedrooms (about 450 sq ft of ceiling) at ₹120/sq ft is ₹54,000. Profile lights, a cove, and a handful of good spotlights add ₹35,000–60,000. If your ceiling height is under 9'6", skip the bedrooms — [we explain why here](/journal/false-ceiling-costs-types-and-when-you-need-one/).

### Painting and electrical: 8–10% each

Painting a 2BHK at ₹32/sq ft of wall area (roughly 2,800 sq ft of wall and ceiling) is ₹90,000 for a premium emulsion. Electrical rewiring with modular switches runs ₹70/sq ft of carpet area — ₹66,000 for 950 sq ft — more if the builder's wiring is undersized, which in Indore flats built before 2018 it very often is.

### Everything else: 15–20%

TV unit, crockery unit, shoe rack, pooja unit, study table, mirrors, curtains, the door for the utility — individually small, collectively a lakh and a half.

The last one is the most common and the most avoidable. Almost every project that overran its budget by more than 10% in our records did so because of changes made after procurement, not because of a wrong estimate.

## What you can safely skip in a 2BHK

- **False ceiling in bedrooms** if the height is tight or you do not need concealed AC ducting.
- **A separate crockery unit** if the kitchen has a tall unit — a 3 ft glass-front section in the kitchen run does the job.
- **Wooden flooring** in a flat where the builder's vitrified tile is in good condition. Use a large rug instead; revisit in five years.
- **Automation** beyond two or three smart switches. Retrofit later; the wiring provision is what matters now.

## How to use these numbers

1. Run [our estimator](/estimate/) with your actual carpet area and tier — it uses this exact price book and shows you the room-wise split.
2. Ask every firm you are talking to for a **per-room, per-item** breakdown with the core material, finish and hardware brand named on every line.
3. Compare rates, not totals. A ₹2,900/rft kitchen in HDHMR with Hettich hardware is a different product from a ₹2,900/rft kitchen in MDF with no-name channels.
4. Hold back 8–10% as a contingency you do not tell anyone about.
